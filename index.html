Leftover Rescue
🌱 Save food • Save money • Meet your neighbours

Good food deserves a second chance.

Leftover Rescue connects bakeries, cafés, home gardeners and neighbours with people nearby who can rescue surplus food before it goes to waste.

How it works

Three simple steps for a local food rescue.

1List
2Rescue
3Enjoy

Example: A bakery has 12 bagels left at 5 PM. It lists a mystery bag for ₹80. Someone nearby reserves it and picks it up before closing.
